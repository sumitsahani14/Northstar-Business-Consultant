About Northstar

Better decisions start with better questions.

Northstar is a fictional boutique advisory firm created as an SS Web Studio portfolio concept. Its point of view: businesses rarely need more noise—they need clarity, focus and practical execution.

The philosophy

Clarity is a competitive advantage.

We believe consulting should leave a leadership team with fewer unanswered questions and a clearer operating rhythm. That means understanding the economics of the business, prioritizing the few moves that matter, and translating strategy into accountable action.

Northstar works across strategy, growth, operations and profitability because these disciplines are connected in the real world.
